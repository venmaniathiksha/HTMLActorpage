Vijay

Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar, known as Vijay, is a prominent Indian actor and politician, celebrated for his significant contributions to Tamil cinema and his recent entry into politics. Early Life Vijay was born on June 22, 1974, in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, to film director S. A. Chandrasekhar and playback singer Shoba Chandrasekhar. He has a younger sister, Vidhya, who passed away at a young age. Vijay began his education at Fathima School and later attended Loyola College, where he pursued a degree in visual communication but left to focus on his acting career. Career in Film Vijay made his acting debut as a child artist in the film Vetri (1984) and transitioned to lead roles with Naalaiya Theerpu (1992). He gained popularity with films like Poove Unakkaga (1996) and Kadhalukku Mariyadhai (1997). Over the years, he starred in numerous successful films, including Ghilli (2004), Pokkiri (2007), Thuppakki (2012), Mersal (2017), and Master (2021). His films are known for their commercial success, and he is regarded as one of the highest-paid actors in India, often referred to by his fans as Thalapathy (Commander).
